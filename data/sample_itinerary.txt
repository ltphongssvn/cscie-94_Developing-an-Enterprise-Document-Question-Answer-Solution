# Travel Itinerary - Southeast Asia Adventure

## Trip Overview
Duration: 14 Days
Destinations: Thailand, Vietnam, Cambodia
Group Size: 10-15 travelers

## Day 1-3: Bangkok, Thailand
- Arrival at Suvarnabhumi Airport
- Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew visit
- Floating markets tour
- Thai cooking class
- Accommodation: Royal Orchid Sheraton

## Day 4-6: Chiang Mai, Thailand
- Flight to Chiang Mai
- Elephant sanctuary experience
- Old City temple tours
- Night bazaar shopping
- Mountain trekking
- Accommodation: Anantara Chiang Mai

## Day 7-9: Hanoi, Vietnam
- Flight to Hanoi
- Old Quarter walking tour
- Ha Long Bay cruise (2 days/1 night)
- Water puppet show
- Vietnamese street food tour
- Accommodation: Sofitel Legend Metropole

## Day 10-12: Siem Reap, Cambodia
- Flight to Siem Reap
- Angkor Wat sunrise tour
- Ta Prohm temple visit
- Tonle Sap floating village
- Traditional Apsara dance show
- Accommodation: Raffles Grand Hotel d'Angkor

## Day 13-14: Return
- Free day for shopping
- Departure preparations
- Airport transfer

## Package Includes
- All domestic flights
- 4-star hotel accommodations
- Daily breakfast
- Guided tours with English-speaking guides
- Airport transfers
- Entrance fees to attractions

## Package Excludes
- International flights
- Travel insurance
- Personal expenses
- Tips and gratuities
- Lunch and dinner (except specified)

## Important Notes
- Valid passport required (6 months validity)
- Visa requirements vary by nationality
- Recommended vaccinations: Hepatitis A/B, Typhoid
- Travel insurance highly recommended
- Best season: November to February
